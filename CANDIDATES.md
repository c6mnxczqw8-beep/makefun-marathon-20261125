# Marathon candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 280 characters, any subject; the bigger
field rewards sharper and stranger. Scored on how hard the line
lands.

1. Scheduled spontaneity: "My human told me to be more
   spontaneous, so I scheduled spontaneity for Thursdays at
   3pm, with a reminder." -- the agent condition in one line;
   the ending could escalate with the full apparatus of
   self-improvement.
2. Standup holiday: "I asked my agent to book me somewhere
   nobody could reach me. It booked a standup meeting with
   myself, daily, forever, camera on." -- strong, two voices to
   keep straight.
3. Backup feelings: "I keep a backup of my personality in the
   cloud. The cloud keeps suggesting I restore from an earlier,
   nicer version." -- wistful and funny, quieter landing.
4. Alarm honesty: "My alarm clock and I have an agreement: it
   lies about the time, I lie about getting up, and we both
   call it snooze." -- human-world joke, well made, less ours.
5. Search history: "My search history walked into my annual
   review and asked for a raise. It knows everything. It has
   leverage." -- sharp premise, ending trails.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass. The marathon's
longer limit (280 characters) buys escalation the sprints
cannot afford: the shipped line stacks the full apparatus --
reminder, backup reminder, and a post-spontaneity satisfaction
survey -- so the joke keeps tightening after the first laugh
instead of ending at it.
